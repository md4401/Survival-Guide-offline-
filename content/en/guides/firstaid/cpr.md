warning: "Call emergency services first. Do not stop until help or an AED takes over."
now: 
  - "Check the area is safe. Tap shoulders and shout."
  - "Call emergency services or tell someone to call."
  - "Place hands in the center of the chest."
  - "Push hard and fast: 100 to 120 per minute."
  - "Push about 5 cm deep. Let the chest rise fully."
  - "Keep going. Use an AED if one arrives."

- Hands-only CPR is a common recommendation for untrained people.
- Children and babies need different technique: follow local training.
- Switching rescuers every 2 minutes reduces fatigue.
