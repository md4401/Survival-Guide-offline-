now:
  - "Sit upright and lean slightly forward."
  - "Pinch the soft part of the nose firmly."
  - "Breathe through the mouth and keep pinching without peeking."
  - "Hold for ten minutes, then check."
  - "Repeat once if it still bleeds."
  - "Rest quietly and do not blow the nose afterwards."
