now:
  - Arrive with at least 2 hours of daylight left.
  - Pick flat, firm, well-drained ground, slightly raised.
  - Stay clear of dead branches and dead trees.
  - Camp about 60 metres from rivers and lakes.
  - Avoid dry riverbeds, valley floors and slope bases.
  - Clear stones and sticks. Spread a ground tarp.
  - Pitch the tent with its door facing away from the wind.
  - Cook and store food 30 metres or more from the tent.
  - Dig the toilet 60 metres or more from water and trails.
  - Before sleeping, keep flashlight and shoes in the same place.

- Wind: pitch with the lowest, narrowest side facing it and use every guy line.
- Rain: slope to shed water and a shallow trench, where allowed, without damaging the ground.
- Heat: shade, cross ventilation and water. Never sleep in a closed car in the sun.
- Cold: the ground steals more heat than the air. Insulate underneath and layer up.
- Lightning: if thunder follows lightning in under 30 seconds, take shelter. Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder.
- Fire: only where permitted. Put it out with water until the ash is cold to the touch.
